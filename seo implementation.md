1. FIRST Understand Search Intent

Before writing ANY page ask:

Why is the user searching this?

There are 4 main intents:

Intent	Example
Informational	“What is SEO?”
Commercial	“Best SEO agency”
Transactional	“Hire SEO expert”
Navigational	“Elementor login”

Your page must match intent exactly.

Example:
If someone searches:

“AI chatbot agency”

Don’t write a blog explaining AI history.

They want:

services
pricing
benefits
examples
trust

Ideal SEO Page Structure

5. Biggest AI Content Mistakes To Avoid

Google does NOT hate AI.

Google hates:

low quality
unhelpful
mass-generated
repetitive
generic content
Avoid These AI Patterns
1. Repetitive Sentences

Example:

“In today’s digital world…”

Used everywhere.

2. Overly Generic Writing

Example:

“SEO is important for businesses.”

No value.

3. Fluffy Introductions

AI loves:

long intros
unnecessary transitions
filler text

Remove fluff.

4. Keyword Stuffing

BAD:

Natural writing wins.

5. Fake Expertise

Avoid fake:

studies
testimonials
6. Robotic Tone

AI often sounds:
